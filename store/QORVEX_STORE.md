**Description**

```
QORVEX brings the joy of newspaper puzzles to learning English vocabulary. Clues are in Turkish, answers are in English, so your vocabulary grows while you solve.

9 NEWSPAPER GAMES
• Arrowword: the classic arrow-clue puzzle of Turkish newspapers
• Picture Puzzle: name the object in the photo at the centre of the grid
• Crossword: the classic numbered puzzle
• Codeword: crack the numbers and read the hidden sentence
• Spiral, Word Search, Word Ladder, Jumble and Cryptogram

VOCABULARY CARDS AND NOTEBOOK
Tap the clue of a word you don't know to open its card with meanings, an example sentence and pronunciation. The word goes into your notebook and returns in later puzzles with spaced repetition. Words you didn't save don't keep repeating.

LEVELS FROM A1 TO C2
Pick your level; each level includes the words of the levels below. Solve 50 puzzles at your level and you move up automatically.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Daily streak, weekly rhythm, fastest times, learned words and hint-free solve rate, all in your Solver Report.

• No account needed, no ads
• Your progress stays on your device
• A new Daily Puzzle every day
```
