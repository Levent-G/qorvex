2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
QORVEX is a newspaper-style English vocabulary puzzle game for Turkish speakers. Clues are written in Turkish and the answers are typed in English, in the style of the classic British "arrowword" newspaper puzzle.
Target audience: Turkish-speaking teenagers and adults who are learning English (levels A1 to C2).
Problem it solves: memorising vocabulary lists is tedious. QORVEX turns vocabulary practice into daily puzzles. When a user opens the vocabulary card of a word they do not know, the word is added to their personal word list and appears again in later puzzles with spaced repetition, so they actually remember it.
Value: free, offline, no ads, no account; more than 4,500 words with meanings and example sentences.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login, credentials or sample files are required.
- Launch the app. The home page shows today's puzzle and the levels (A1 to C2 and Mixed).
- Tap any level to open a puzzle. Tap a white square and type the English answer with the on-screen keyboard. Tapping the same square again switches between across and down.
- When a word is complete it turns green (correct), yellow (close) or red (wrong).
- Tap a grey clue square once to select its word, and tap it again (or tap the clue bar above the keyboard) to open the vocabulary card with pronunciation, Turkish meanings and example sentences.
- The light bulb button at the top right offers hints (reveal a letter, reveal a word, clear a word).
- After solving a puzzle, tap "Next" for a new one. "Back" at the top left returns to the home page.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app works completely offline and makes no network requests. It does not use any analytics, advertising, authentication, payment or AI services. Puzzles are generated on the device from a word database bundled in the app. Word pronunciation uses Apple's built-in on-device text-to-speech. Progress is stored only in local storage on the device. The app is built with React Native / Expo.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
